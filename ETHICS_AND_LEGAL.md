# Ethical and Legal Assessment

## Ethical Considerations

### Transparency

This project clearly informs users that AI was used during development.

### Reliability

AI-generated information may contain errors. Users should verify important legal information using reliable sources.

### Human Oversight

All AI-generated content was reviewed and edited before inclusion in the final project.

### Accessibility

The project is designed with simple language to make legal concepts easier to understand.

## Legal Considerations
